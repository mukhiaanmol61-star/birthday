A warm hug from me · 🫂

For Your Difficult Days

My love, I know there are days when you are tired in ways that sleep cannot completely fix. Days when things feel unfair, when you worry about tomorrow, or when you wish life could simply be a little easier.

On those days, please remember that you don't have to pretend to be okay for me. You don't have to smile just to reassure me. You don't have to hide your fears or your tears.

You are allowed to rest. You are allowed to feel. You are allowed to have difficult days without feeling guilty.

I may not always know the perfect words. I may not be able to solve every problem. But I want to listen, understand, support you and remind you that you are not defined by what you are going through.

I see your courage, but I also want you to know that you don't have to be brave every minute. You can lean on me too.

I hope life becomes gentler with you. I hope better days find you. And until they do, I hope you always remember how deeply you are loved.

I am here, beside you. 🫂

Words from my heart · 💌

A Letter From My Heart

My love,

Sometimes I sit and think about how much you have become a part of my life. It feels strange how a person can slowly become so important that their happiness, their comfort, their dreams and their little moments begin to matter so deeply to you.

You are that person for me.

I know our life isn't a fairytale where everything is perfect. We have our own worries, responsibilities, difficult moments and things we wish we could change. But even in an imperfect life, loving you is one of the things that feels right to me.

I want to be there for your happy moments. I want to celebrate your achievements. I want to hear about the little things that happened in your day. I want to laugh with you about things that probably make no sense to anyone else.

And when life becomes difficult, I want to be someone you can talk to without fear of being judged. Someone who listens. Someone who respects your feelings. Someone who doesn't make you feel like a burden for needing love or reassurance.

I want to keep learning how to love you better. Not just through words, but through patience, effort, understanding and the little things I do every day.

You matter to me more than I can properly explain. I hope you never forget that, especially on days when you don't feel your best.

Thank you for being a part of my life. Thank you for the memories we already have. Thank you for all the memories waiting for us.

Happy birthday, my beautiful girl. I wish you everything good that this world can offer.

With all my love,
Yours. 🤎
